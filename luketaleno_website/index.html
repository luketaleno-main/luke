The Story

From the seat to the stage

A year before his audition, Luke Taleno sat in the America's Got Talent crowd and told Simon Cowell to watch for him — he'd be back for a Golden Buzzer. He delivered: performing his original "Seat to Stage" and flipping mid-song into Spanish for Sofía Vergara, he earned Howie Mandel's first Golden Buzzer of the season.

Born in Cuba and raised in Hialeah, Florida, Luke is a bilingual artist and storyteller who turns real life into music — and he bet everything on it, quitting his 9-to-5 with no backup plan. "Seat to Stage" is out now on all platforms, and the Golden Buzzer sends him straight to the Live Shows.

"You are truly the image of what I believe authenticity is."— Howie Mandel, on giving Luke the Golden Buzzer
