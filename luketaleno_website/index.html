The Story

Real life, turned into music

Born in Cuba and raised in Hialeah, Florida, Luke Taleno is a bilingual artist and storyteller who turns real life into songs. A childhood cancer survivor, he learned early that time is the one thing you can't get back — so he bet everything on his music, quitting his 9-to-5 with no backup plan.

That leap paid off. He earned Howie Mandel's Golden Buzzer on America's Got Talent and took his story to the national stage, then brought that momentum straight back home to Miami. Today he's building on it with new music, live shows, and community work across South Florida — freestyling, performing, and telling his city's story every chance he gets.

"You are truly the image of what I believe authenticity is."— Howie Mandel, on giving Luke the Golden Buzzer
